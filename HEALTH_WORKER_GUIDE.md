# Sahayak: Health Worker Clinical Guide 🩺

Welcome to **Sahayak**, your digital clinical partner. This guide will help you use the dashboard to manage your village's health, follow up with patients, and coordinate with doctors.

---

## 1. The Proactive Task List (Your Daily To-Do) 🔔
On the left sidebar, you will see the **Proactive Outreach Tasks**. These are patients who need your attention *before* they get sick:
*   **Red Alerts**: Patients who missed their critical medication doses.
*   **Blue Alerts**: Patients who haven't logged their BP or Blood Sugar for over 48 hours.
*   **Action**: Click "Follow up" to call or visit the patient and update their status.

---

## 2. Voice-Powered Clinical Documentation 🎙️
You don't need to fill out long forms. In the main console, you can dictate your visit outcomes:
*   **How to do it**: Click the Microphone icon and say: *"I visited Meena today. Her BP is 130/85, her weight is 65kg, and she is taking her medicines regularly."*
*   **Result**: The AI will automatically extract the numbers and save them to her health record.

---

## 3. Clinical Protocols (Ask the AI) 🧠
If you are unsure about a clinical procedure, ask the AI directly:
*   *"What is the dosage of Zinc for a child with diarrhea?"*
*   *"What are the signs of high-risk pregnancy in the 7th month?"*
*   **Result**: The AI will provide evidence-based advice grounded in official National Health Mission (NHM) guidelines.

---

## 4. Tele-Consultation Bridge (For Doctors) 📞
If a patient in the field has a high-risk symptom (e.g., chest pain), they will appear in the **Live Consultations Queue**.
*   **Action**: Doctors can click **"Join Consultation"** to instantly enter a high-fidelity voice call with the citizen and the ASHA worker.
*   **Feature**: The AI remains in the background to transcribe the doctor's advice and generate a digital prescription automatically.

---

## 5. Performance & Gamification 🏆
The **Worker Leaderboard** tracks your activity across the district:
*   **10 Points**: For every new clinical record created.
*   **5 Points**: For every unique patient you follow up with.
*   **Goal**: Reach the Top 10 to receive district-level recognition and performance incentives.

---

## 6. Tips for Best Results 💡
1.  **Speak Naturally**: The AI understands regional dialects (Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Bhojpuri). You don't need to use perfect medical terms.
2.  **High Noise**: If you are in a noisy area, use the "Micro-Voice" mode (Push-to-Talk) to ensure the AI captures your voice clearly.
3.  **Sync**: If you are in a zero-signal area, your records are saved locally and will upload automatically once you reach a signal zone.
