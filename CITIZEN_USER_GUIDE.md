# Welcome to Aarogya: Your Voice Health Assistant 🎙️

Hello! I am **Aarogya**, your AI Health Assistant. You don't need to type or click buttons—you can just talk to me, and I will help you with your health needs.

---

## 1. How to Start Talking to Me 🗣️
I am always listening for my "Wake Word." To start a conversation, just say:
*   **"Namaste"** (Hindi/Bhojpuri)
*   **"Hello"** or **"Hi"** (English)
*   **"Vanakkam"** (Tamil)
*   **"Namaskara"** (Kannada)

Once the green light on the screen starts pulsing, I am listening!

---

## 2. I Speak Your Language 🇮🇳
You don't need to worry about English. I can understand and speak:
*   **Hindi** (नमस्ते)
*   **Bhojpuri** (प्रणाम)
*   **Tamil** (வணக்கம்)
*   **Kannada** (ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ)
*   **English**

---

## 3. What Can I Do for You? 🏥
You can ask me many things about your health:

### 🗓️ Book an Appointment
Just say: *"I want to see a doctor at the PHC tomorrow morning."* 
I will book your time and send an SMS to your phone.

### 🌡️ Check Your Symptoms
If you feel unwell, say: *"I have had a high fever and a cough for two days."* 
I will check if it's serious and tell you what to do next.

### 💊 Track Your Medicines
Say: *"I took my blood pressure medicine today"* or *"What was my BP last week?"*
I keep a record of your health so you don't have to.

### 📜 Ayushman Bharat & Schemes
Ask: *"Am I eligible for Ayushman Bharat?"* 
I will help you check your benefits using your phone number or ABHA ID.

---

## 4. Example Conversations 💬

**Example 1: Booking a Doctor**
*   **You**: "Namaste. I need to book an appointment."
*   **Aarogya**: "Namaste! I can help with that. Which clinic would you like to visit, and when?"
*   **You**: "The village PHC, on Monday at 10 AM."
*   **Aarogya**: "Done! I have booked your slot for Monday, 10 AM at the PHC. You will receive an SMS shortly."

**Example 2: Reporting Vitals (BP)**
*   **You**: "I just checked my blood pressure. It is 140 over 90."
*   **Aarogya**: "Thank you. I have recorded your BP as 140/90. This is a bit high. Please make sure to drink water and I will alert the ASHA worker to check on you tomorrow."

---

## 5. Important Safety Note ⚠️
*   **If it is a life-threatening emergency** (like chest pain or heavy bleeding), do not wait for me. **Go to the nearest hospital or PHC immediately.**
*   I am an assistant, not a doctor. I help you connect to the right care at the right time.

---

## 6. Your Privacy 🔒
Your health information is safe with us. We only share your data with verified government health workers (like your local ASHA or Doctor) to help you get better.
